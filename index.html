AEMO MLF Tracker

How much of the regional price does each asset lose on the way in, and is it getting worse? A marginal loss factor (MLF) is the share of the regional price a DUID receives: 1.0000 means no loss, 0.9000 means 10% lost.

Generators

One row per DUID that is not a battery. Each cell is the DUID's MLF for that financial year: the share of the regional price it receives, so 1.0000 means no loss. YoY is the latest MLF minus the one before (a fall means more price lost) and YoY % is the same change as a percentage. Click a column to sort; the default is the latest year with the deepest loss first.

Battery Storage

Batteries are DUIDs with fuel category Battery. Cells work as in the generator table. The columns marked Import are the MLF applied when the battery draws power from the grid to charge: a factor on the price it pays, not the price it receives. It carries no heat fill, because a low import MLF means the battery charges more cheaply — the opposite of a loss.
